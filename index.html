Know your rights

4 things recovery agents hope you never find out

01

They can't call you whenever they like

Recovery calls are expected to be made at reasonable hours, generally 8 AM to 7 PM.

02

Threats and abuse are not allowed

RBI's fair-practice norms prohibit intimidation, abusive language and public humiliation.

03

Missing a credit card bill isn't a crime

Non-payment of unsecured loans is generally a civil matter, not a reason for arrest.

04

A legal notice is not the end

It's the start of a process. Responding correctly and on time is what matters.
